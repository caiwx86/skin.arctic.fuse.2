Customize the teather splash

The default filename the theater splash bg when play begin is: bh2.png
The default filename the theater splash image when play begin is: h3.png

When want to choose another, just rename the bh2.png to bh2.png.old, and h3.png to h3.png.old


Then choose another image bg from here, like : bh3.png , then rename it to bh2.png 
Then choose another image splash from here, like: h7.png , then rename it to h3.png

Reboot the skin or kodi.
